I'm heartbroken to hear about the cancellation of the Grace Hopper Celebration.

Exactly 8 years ago, I attended as a scholar, and that experience changed the trajectory of my life. The relationships I built, the mentors I met, and the opportunities that followed helped shape the leader I am today. Conferences like these are not just events. They are catalysts for careers, confidence, community, and innovation.

That's why this news hits so hard.

Across the country, organizations dedicated to creating access, connection, and leadership opportunities are facing growing challenges. ATHENA International is experiencing many of the same headwinds. Yet at a time when our communities face increasing division, uncertainty, and environmental challenges, we need these spaces more than ever.

We cannot afford to stop bringing people together.
